## Module 1: THE BRIDGE (Intelligence Layer)

### 8 Continuous Monitoring Checks
1. **OOS Detection** - Products out of stock but ads still running
2. **Wasted Spend Analysis** - Products with spend but zero sales
3. **ROAS by Product** - What's actually profitable at SKU level
4. **Creative Fatigue Detection** - Ads burning out (frequency, CTR decline)
5. **Catalog Sync Issues** - Meta vs Shopify mismatches
6. **Budget Pacing** - Over/under spending against targets
7. **Audience Saturation** - Frequency too high across ad sets
8. **Competitor Price Monitoring** - Being undercut on key products

### System Outputs
- Real-time alerts via WhatsApp/Email
- "Stop spending on X" automated actions
- "Double down on Y" recommendations
- Product prioritization fed to Creative Engine

---

## Module 2: CREATIVE ENGINE (Production Layer)

### What It Produces (Based on Bridge Data)
1. **UGC Videos** - Product demos, testimonials, unboxing
2. **Static Ads** - Product shots, lifestyle backgrounds
3. **Carousels** - Multi-product, story-driven
4. **Ad Copy** - Headlines, descriptions, CTAs with A/B variants

### The Key: DATA-DRIVEN Production
- Bridge: "Champagne Gold Sharara has 8.42x ROAS"
- Engine: Auto-generates content for it
- Bridge: "Red Ajrakh has 0 ROAS, Rs 18K wasted"
- Engine: STOPS producing for it, reallocates to winners

**The system decides. You approve.**
