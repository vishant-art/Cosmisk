# Creative Brief: Smashed Agency Ad Scripts

> **For:** Partner creating video ads for smashed.agency/scan/
> **Goal:** Scripts that sell the SYSTEM, not creatives

---

## What We Sell (One Sentence)

**A performance intelligence system that runs 24/7 - watching your ad data, predicting when ads will die, and producing replacements before you even notice.**

---

## The Problem We Solve (Use in Scripts)

### Pain Point 1: "Always 3 Days Late"
> "Your ads die every 5-7 days. You find out 3 days AFTER performance tanks. By then, you've wasted Rs 40K-1L on dead creatives."

**Emotion:** Frustration, anxiety, feeling behind

### Pain Point 2: "Constant Firefighting"
> "Your team spends 8-10 hours every week analyzing dashboards, checking what's working, figuring out what to make next. You're always reacting, never ahead."

**Emotion:** Exhaustion, overwhelm

### Pain Point 3: "Guessing Game"
> "You don't know which products to promote. You don't know which formats work. You're guessing - and 20-30% of your spend is wasted on wrong bets."

**Emotion:** Uncertainty, fear of wasting money

---

## The Solution (What We Install)

### NOT "We make 50 creatives/month"
### YES "We install a system that runs 24/7"

**The 6 Functions (Use These in Scripts):**

| Function | Script-Friendly Description |
|----------|----------------------------|
| 24/7 Monitoring | "It watches your ads while you sleep" |
| Fatigue Prediction | "Alerts you 48-72 hours BEFORE the drop" |
| Pattern Learning | "Learns what works for YOUR audience" |
| Brief Generation | "Tells you exactly what to make next" |
| Auto Production | "Creates the creatives automatically" |
| Approval Workflow | "You spend 30 minutes a week approving" |

## Script Frameworks

### Framework 1: Problem-Agitate-Solve (30 sec)

**Problem (5 sec):**
"Your ads die every 5-7 days."

**Agitate (10 sec):**
"You find out 3 days late. Rs 40K wasted. Team scrambling. Again."

**Solve (15 sec):**
"What if a system predicted fatigue 48 hours ahead and created replacements automatically? That's what we install. You approve. It handles the rest."

---

### Framework 2: Before/After (30 sec)

**Before (10 sec):**
"Before: 10 hours a week staring at dashboards. Guessing what to make. Finding out ads died yesterday."

**After (10 sec):**
"After: 30 minutes a week approving. System tells you what's working. Alerts you before anything drops."

**CTA (10 sec):**
"We install this system for brands spending Rs 10L+/month. Free strategy session - link in bio."

---

### Framework 3: "Did You Know?" Hook (15 sec)

**Hook (3 sec):**
"Did you know 20-30% of your ad spend is wasted?"

**Insight (7 sec):**
"You're promoting products with zero ROAS. Running ads on out-of-stock items. Nobody's watching."

**CTA (5 sec):**
"We find the waste. We fix it. Free audit - link below."

---

### Framework 4: Founder Talking Head (45 sec)

**Intro (5 sec):**
"If you're spending Rs 10L+ on Meta ads, watch this."

**Problem (15 sec):**
"Your creatives burn out in 3-7 days. By the time you notice, you've wasted lakhs. Your team spends 10 hours a week just analyzing data. And you're still guessing what to make next."

**Solution (15 sec):**
"We built a system that monitors your ads 24/7, predicts fatigue 48 hours ahead, and produces replacements automatically. You spend 30 minutes a week approving. The system does everything else."

**Proof (5 sec):**
"One client found Rs 5.8 lakh in wasted spend in their first week."

**CTA (5 sec):**
"Book a free strategy session. We'll audit your account - no strings attached."
