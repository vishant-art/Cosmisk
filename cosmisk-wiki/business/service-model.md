# Service Model

> How we sell. Two tiers, service-first (not SaaS).

## The Hierarchy

```
SMASHED AGENCY (smashed.agency)
└── The brand clients see and pay

THE BRIDGE SERVICE (smashed.agency/scan/)
└── The service clients buy
└── "We watch The Gap between your platforms"

COSMISK (this codebase)
└── Backend platform clients NEVER see
```

## Two Service Tiers

### Done With You (Rs 3-5L one-time) — HIGHER TICKET
- **Who:** Founder who wants to own the system
- **What:** We implement + train their team
- **Duration:** 60-day implementation + 30-day support
- **After:** They run independently, no dependency
- **Why expensive:** Knowledge transfer + independence = premium

### Done For You (Rs 50-75K/month) — RECURRING
- **Who:** Time-poor founder, wants hands-off
- **What:** We handle everything continuously
- **Duration:** Ongoing monthly
- **After:** Stay dependent on us
- **Why cheaper per month:** Ongoing relationship, no knowledge transfer

## Target Client

- Rs 10L+/month ad spend
- D2C brand (fashion, beauty, home)
- Already running Meta Ads
- Wants strategic partnership, not tools

## Key Decisions Made

- No public pricing (discovery call first)
- WhatsApp-first alerts (not dashboards)
- Position as "intelligence layer" not "tool"
- Service first, SaaS after service proven
