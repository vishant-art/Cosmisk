# Patterns: What Fails

> Anti-patterns to avoid. Learn from past mistakes.

## Hook Anti-Patterns

### Urgency Hooks (for low-trust audiences)
- **Avg Click-to-ATC**: 0.9%
- **Why fails**: Trust < 60 audiences see urgency as manipulation
- **Example**: "Only 3 left!", "Sale ends tonight!"
- **When OK**: Trust > 70, most-aware audiences only

### Discount-Led
- **Problem**: High CTR but low ATC
- **Why fails**: Attracts price-shoppers, not brand believers
- **Result**: Bait clicks, low conversion, poor LTV
- **When OK**: Clearance, retention campaigns only

## Visual Anti-Patterns

### Studio Polish (for skeptical audiences)
- **Trust drop**: ~15%
- **Why fails**: Feels inauthentic, "too good to be true"
- **Alternative**: Documentary authentic style

### Stock Imagery
- **Why fails**: Generic, no differentiation
- **Alternative**: Real product, real people, real environment

## Messaging Anti-Patterns

### Generic Claims
- **Example**: "Best quality", "Premium product"
- **Why fails**: Every brand says this
- **Alternative**: Specific proof, specific numbers

### Feature Dumping
- **Why fails**: Audience doesn't care about specs
- **Alternative**: Benefits, outcomes, transformation

### Pain Amplification (for fatigued audiences)
- **Why fails**: If exhausted, creates negative association
- **Alternative**: Shift to proof, benefits, education

## Operational Anti-Patterns

### Scaling Too Fast
- **Problem**: >30%/week budget increase
- **Result**: Performance drop, CPA spike
- **Rule**: Max 20-30% weekly scaling

### Ignoring Fatigue Signals
- **Problem**: Keep running hook after CTR decline starts
- **Result**: 2+ weeks of wasted spend
- **Rule**: Detect at 48 hours, act within 7 days
