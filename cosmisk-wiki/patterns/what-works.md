# Patterns: What Works

> Cross-client winning patterns. Updated as we learn.

## Hook Patterns

### Founder Intro
- **Avg Click-to-ATC**: 4.2%
- **Best for**: Trust-building, skeptical audiences
- **Opening**: Founder visible, eye contact, authentic setting
- **Avoid**: Studio polish, scripted feel
- **Clients using**: [[clients/pratapsons]]

### Process Revelation
- **Avg Click-to-ATC**: 3.8%
- **Best for**: Premium positioning, differentiation
- **Opening**: Hands at work, raw materials visible
- **Avoid**: Finished product hero shot
- **Clients using**: [[clients/pratapsons]]

### Customer Story
- **Avg Click-to-ATC**: 3.5%
- **Best for**: Social proof, consideration stage
- **Opening**: Real customer, authentic environment
- **Avoid**: Actors, over-polished testimonials

## Visual Patterns

### Documentary Authentic
- **Best for**: Trust crisis, skepticism
- **Style**: Natural light, real environment, imperfections OK
- **Avoid**: Studio polish, stock imagery

### Brand Elevated
- **Best for**: High trust, product-aware audiences
- **Style**: Professional but warm
- **Avoid**: Cheap looking, generic stock

## CTA Patterns

### Soft Invitation
- **Text**: "See our process", "Learn more"
- **Best for**: Trust < 60, early funnel
- **Avoid**: "Shop now", "Limited time"

### Clear Action
- **Text**: "Shop now", "Get yours"
- **Best for**: Trust > 70, most-aware audiences

## Emotional Patterns

### Reassurance
- **Best for**: Trust crisis
- **Journey**: doubt → openness → reassurance → consideration

### Curiosity
- **Best for**: Skepticism
- **Journey**: skepticism → curiosity → trust → desire
