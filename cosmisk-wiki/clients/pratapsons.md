## What Works
- **Founder intro hooks**: 4.2% click-to-ATC (vs 1.8% avg)
- **Process revelation**: Shows artisan craftsmanship
- **Natural lighting**: Documentary style > studio polish
- **Soft CTAs**: "See our process" > "Shop now"

## What Doesn't Work
- **Urgency hooks**: 0.9% click-to-ATC (audience resistant)
- **Discount-led**: High CTR but low ATC (bait clicks)
- **Studio polish**: Trust drops 15% (feels inauthentic)

## Current Creatives
- Founder workshop video (15s): RUNNING, performing well
- Process reveal carousel: RUNNING, performing well
- Urgency sale ad: PAUSED after poor performance

## Operational Constraints
- Founder availability: 2 hours/week
- Production capacity: 3 videos/week
- Budget phase: Scaling

## Strategic Direction
Trust-building first. Founder transparency as primary leverage. Avoid urgency tactics until trust score > 70.
