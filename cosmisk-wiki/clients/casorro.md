# Client: Casorro

> Case study for content. Real data.

## The Story

**Jan-Feb:** Rs 1,200 CPA. Profitable.
**Mar-Apr:** Rs 7,650 CPA. Burning cash.

Same product. Same audience. Same landing page.

## Root Cause

Budget shifted from winning UGC to new template creatives.

## The Data

| Creative Type | Spend | Purchases | Click→ATC | CPA |
|---------------|-------|-----------|-----------|-----|
| UGC (winner) | Rs 1,07,874 | 127 | 5.26% | Rs 849 |
| Templates (tests) | Rs 1,05,128 | 6 | 0.17% | Rs 17,521 |

**Key Insight:** 31x difference in Click-to-ATC rate. Same spend.

## Lesson

Not "creative fatigue" — new tests were bad from day 1.
Click-to-ATC reveals this in 48 hours, not months.
