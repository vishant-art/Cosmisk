What we found · last 30 days

Your biggest spend is arguing with your positioning.

The ads taking most of your budget lead with price. Your brand leads with barrier repair. Those two things are pulling in different directions.

What we understand about your brand
  • Premium position — products ₹1,450–₹3,200
  • Promise: repair the barrier, not treat symptoms (you set this)
  • Ceramide-first, fragrance-free, derm-tested
What we see in your account
  • ₹4.1L of ₹6.0L spend on 4 discount-led creatives
  • Those 4 blend to 2.3× ROAS
  • 2 barrier-repair creatives: ₹0.9L at 3.1× ROAS
Read together
Fact — what we observed

68% of your spend in the last 30 days (₹4.1L of ₹6.0L) ran on four creatives whose opening line is a discount — “FLAT 40% OFF”, “LOWEST PRICE EVER”. Those four blend to a 2.3× ROAS. The two creatives that open with a barrier-repair claim took ₹0.9L and returned 3.1×.

Inference — how we read it

You charge roughly three times the category average and your stated promise is barrier repair, so most of your budget is currently teaching new customers to buy you on price — while the creative that actually matches your positioning is the one performing better on a much smaller budget. The split looks inherited rather than chosen.

Recommendation — what to do in the next 48 hours

Before scaling further, move a defined share of the discount budget — start with 20% — onto the barrier-repair set and compare over two weeks. This is a reallocation test, not a rewrite. Don't pause the discount creatives; you need the comparison.

Why this needed your brand context
Without brand context

“Your discount-led ads have a lower ROAS than your other ads.”

True, generic, and visible in Meta Ads Manager. Any account could receive it.

With brand context

“Most of your budget is teaching customers to buy a premium barrier-repair brand on price.”

Only true for a brand at your price point making your promise.

Where we'd stop

If your website hadn't given us a clear promise, or if these creatives had fewer than about 40 conversions each, we'd tell you there isn't enough evidence to conclude anything — and say what would change that. We'd rather say nothing than say something confident and wrong.
